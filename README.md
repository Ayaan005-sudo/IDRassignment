# Institute of Digital Risk (IDR) – Homepage

## Project Overview

This project is a responsive homepage designed for the **Institute of Digital Risk (IDR)**, an industry-led training and deployment institute focused on **digital, cyber, and AI risk**.
The goal of the website is to clearly communicate IDR’s mission, services, and community while maintaining a clean and professional technology-focused design.

The homepage highlights IDR’s role in training digital risk professionals and connecting **academic research with real-world industry practice**.

---

## Logo

A simple cube-inspired geometric logo was designed to represent **structure, resilience, and digital risk frameworks**.
The colour palette uses **orange, black, and white**, symbolising innovation, professionalism, and clarity.

Two variants were created:

* **Icon-only logo**
* **Icon + text (“Institute of Digital Risk”)**

The logo is designed to remain **clear and legible at small sizes**, making it suitable for use in navigation bars, mobile headers, and favicons.

---

## Homepage Sections

### 1. Hero Section

The hero section introduces IDR’s mission with a clear headline and supporting description.
It includes a **call-to-action button** encouraging users to explore programs or engage with the institute.

### 2. About IDR

This section explains IDR as an **industry-led training and deployment institute** that prepares professionals for roles in digital, cyber, and technology risk.
It highlights the collaboration between **academic insight (UK university partnerships)** and **real-world industry projects**.

### 3. Service Pillars

The core IDR model is presented using cards:

* **Academy** – Training programs and bootcamps for students and professionals.
* **Innovation & Incubation** – Research and development of digital risk models and AI governance frameworks.
* **Advisory Services** – Supporting organisations in implementing frameworks such as **NIST, ISO 27001, and NIS2**.

### 4. Community

This section describes the IDR community, including **students, early-career professionals, and industry practitioners** working in sectors like financial services and critical infrastructure who want to build expertise in digital and cyber risk.

### 5. Contact / Call to Action

A simple contact form allows users to **register their interest or connect with IDR**.
The form collects:

* Name
* Email
* Message

---

## Technologies Used

* **HTML5** – Semantic structure for accessibility and clarity.
* **CSS3** – Custom styling and responsive layout.
* **JavaScript** – Smooth scrolling and basic interactive behaviour.
* **Flexbox** – Responsive layout for cards and sections.
* **Google Fonts** – Clean and modern typography.

No CSS frameworks (such as Bootstrap) were used. The layout is built using **vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**.

---

## Features

* Responsive design (desktop and mobile)
* Sticky navigation bar
* Smooth scrolling between sections
* Accessible colour contrast
* Hover effects for buttons and navigation links
* Card-based layout for service pillars

---

## Purpose of the Project

This project demonstrates the ability to design and develop a **clean, responsive landing page** for an educational and technology-focused organisation using only **core web technologies**.
