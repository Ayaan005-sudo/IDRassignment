ABOUT US

What We Do

The Institute of Digital Risk (IDR) is an industry-led training and deployment institute focused on preparing professionals for roles in digital, cyber, and technology risk. We work in regulated and high-consequence environments where organisations must manage complex digital threats and governance challenges.

IDR combines academic insight through partnerships with leading UK universities with hands-on industry projects. Our model connects education, innovation and real-world deployment to develop skilled digital risk practitioners who can support organisations in building resilient and secure digital systems.

Digital Risk Awareness

We help individuals and organisations understand the evolving landsape of digital risks and online threats through research,insights and practical knowledge.

Cybersecurity training

Our programs and workshops equip professionals with the skills needed to identify,vulnerabilites manage digital threats and build stronger cybersecurity practices.

Global Collaboration

We bring together experts,researchers and professionsals to share knowledge discuss emerging risks and develop innovative solutions for a safer digital ecosyste.
